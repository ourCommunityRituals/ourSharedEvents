## Choice-Infra Coherence Verdict

INCOMPLETE and INCONSISTENT. The Anonymous/Identity/Bridge contract is NOT enforced completely or symmetrically across brain<->OSE<->OW. Strengths are real and should be preserved: Invariant #22 cross-repo import isolation is a genuine fail-closed, registry-derived, brain-resident guard with an isolated REQUIRED job in OSE, and OSE's geo #2 refusal + public projection allowlist + two-DB split are enforced by construction. But the contract fails on three axes. (1) Single-sided enforcement: #22 blocks verifiably only on OSE (OW has no dedicated required inv22 job and no committed branch-protection); #15 CORS immutability is a named brain invariant + L0 gate on OSE but only a repo-local test on the credential-bearing OW pole; and the entire Named I-1..I-8 contract has NO brain check while the Anonymous A-1..A-8 is machine-enforced only as a 3-string grep (CHOICE-02). (2) Documentation overstates enforcement: CHOICE-INVARIANTS.md's 'CI validates it, every commit, every product, every time' is contradicted by the full brain-conformance job being deliberately non-required, the four marquee runtime invariants (#1-#4) gating no PR, A-5 encryption-at-rest and A-8 bucketing having no PR-blocking gate, and A-8's stated 15-min window being hour-bucketing in code. (3) The data layer breaks the anonymity/retention covenant in code: cleartext attendee_name (A-5), incomplete survey scrub with no expiry (A-4), creation-keyed TTL contradicting GDPR notices, unfloored sub-K counts to non-owners (DS-01), and a raw-row BigQuery backup that bypasses the fail-closed pool contract (DS-02). The Bridge/OA pole is effectively undeclared/unverifiable (OAW is a pre-tech stub with contradictory identity vocabulary and no code to scan). Net: the enforcement architecture is sophisticated and mostly sound at the import-isolation firewall, but the choice contract's substantive privacy guarantees are enforced by prose and file-existence, not by blocking behavioral gates, and the enforcement is asymmetric between the anonymous and named poles.

Loop 4 synthesis of 48 confirmed, adversarially-verified findings across brain (OCR-docs), OSE (anonymous pole), and OW (named pole).
