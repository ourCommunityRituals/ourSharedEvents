**Frozen subject:** `main` at `8dbc0a08b6552ff4b2adf03620b31e138fb61cdd` (2026-11-19, "Landing distance: five Within N mi chips (#1480)"). Not moved during the run. Nothing in the product was changed.

**Run model:** the owner's (feedback_ccr_run_model): 5 loops on one frozen subject, nothing changed between loops, one cumulative score at the end, under 95% the team works out solutions. Loop 1 Critical (surface), loop 2 Conservative (verify and pressure), loop 3 Adversarial (solutioning, then attack the solutions), loop 4 Compliance (invariants and design rules), loop 5 Recursive (re-score, discovery rate).

## Scope

In scope: the three journeys as built on the frozen commit, read from templates, handlers, tests and the live staging site.

1. Visitor: landing and `/map`, search, filters, distance chips, `/events`, event page, register, check-in, survey, updates, shortlinks, TV and poster seen from a phone.
2. Organizer: sign-in, `/admin` front door, create wizard, event cockpit and its cards, poster and QR, sponsors, TV, SharedReport (mint and signed link), Logs, Settings.
3. Sponsor: `/sponsor?t=` home, the consent step, sponsor requests.

Out of scope: product code changes (fixes are separate stories), production, the Liz showcase, a re-test of the unmerged work (none is left).

## Method and its limits (read this before the score)

- Seven role agents ran in parallel for loop 1: Sr Design FE, Lead FE Integration twice (visitor and sponsor, organizer), Product Owner, Sr Automation QA, Sr Data Scientist, Dr of Security. Each was read-only and listed its commands.
- **Staging edge:** Cloudflare answers `curl` with a "Just a moment" challenge (403) on `/events/:id`, its forms, `/map`, `/es/*`, `/q /s /e /r /c`, `/sponsor` and `/sponsors/:id`. The agents did not evade it, so those pages are source-read. The owner-side browser pane passed the challenge, so the lead checked some of them (marked `proven (browser)`).
- **Admin** is behind Cloudflare Access. No signed-in session was available, so every admin finding is source-read plus the Playwright specs. One security-lens `curl` of `/admin` was unauthenticated and drew a 302 to the Access login; it changed nothing.
- No test suite was run. No born-red claim is replayed.
- Basis tags: `proven` (seen on staging, in a browser or in HTML) and `read` (source inference). About two thirds of the register is `read`.

## Loop 1: Critical (surface pass, seven lenses)

**Critical question:** what is the most dangerous assumption? That a screen exists in `main` because a canvas board or a story says so. Several promised steps were never built (survey link, the second door, the 3-step wizard, sponsors in the wizard), and several checks that look like coverage run nowhere (F-43, F-45).

**Merging rule:** a defect seen by several lenses is counted once by root cause. About 100 raw findings became 50. Severity weights are the protocol's (Regulatory 25, Critical 15, High 10, Medium 5, Low 2). Heuristic 4 (same defect in 3 or more places promotes one tier) is applied where marked `H4`. Loop 1 position weight is 1.0. Staging-only environment weight (0.75) is not applied, so the score is not flattered.
