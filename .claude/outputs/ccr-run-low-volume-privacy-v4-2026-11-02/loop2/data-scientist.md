## 3. Correlation clusters

- **C1, scan order is one global join key.** R1-01, R1-10 and R1-16 (and the platform log, F-15) are one root: D1 rowid, KV expiry, queue order and log time all follow scan time. Any two join. One store holds (browser, hour) and another holds (poster, second), so together they hold (browser, poster, second). The forms `source_placement_id` and hour (R1-01, S7) share the poster and hour key with D1. With one scan and one registration in a cell, a name attaches to the 365-day cross-event trail. Score this cluster Critical via the correlation modifier. S2 hashing, S3 and S4 touch none of these links. The channel the subject does not name is order itself.
- **C2, none-versus-small bit on public surfaces.** R1-04, L2-DS-1, R1-03 and R1-13 are separate vocabularies ("held", "hidden", "fewer than 5"). Compared across surfaces they give the bit twice.
- **C3, scans are not people.** R1-07, R1-08, L2-DS-2 and the `/q` robots story erode "K scans = K people" together. A broken marker goes undetected.
- **C4, differencing outside the grain.** R1-02, R1-04 and L2-DS-4 are the same family: figures over one population cut at different grains.
