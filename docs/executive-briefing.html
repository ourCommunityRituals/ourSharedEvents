Section 1

The Vision

OurCommunityRituals is building community infrastructure that compounds. Not an app. Not a marketplace. An interconnected platform of products that serve the hyperlocal economy — the neighborhood bar hosting trivia, the church opening its doors for dinner, the rec league that brings strangers together.

Each product in the ecosystem generates data. That data feeds the next product. The more communities use it, the smarter and more valuable the entire system becomes. This is the moat — and it deepens with every event, every scan, every meal shared.

Section 6

The Moat

Six engineering decisions that create initial advantage and compound over time. These are the differentiators.

Anonymous-First Data

No personal data required for core flows. Privacy is the product feature, not the compliance burden. Customers trust it because we never had their data to lose.

3-Tier Feature Flags

Free / Mid / Enterprise. One codebase serves a neighborhood bar and a national franchise. Same QR system, same form quality at every tier. Tiers unlock operational tools, never degrade customer experience.

Brand Isolation

Dual-key pattern in every database query. Each sponsor sees only their data. Multi-tenant from day one — not bolted on later.

Universal Event Codes

Seven color-coded QR codes per event. One scan to register, check in, take a survey, or sign up. Physical-to-digital bridge that works at every scale.

Phased Architecture

Google Sheets → Edge SQLite (D1) → Firebase Auth. Crawl-walk-run without rewrites. Ship now, scale later, never rebuild.

White-Label at Scale

ourSharedMagnets provisions branded front-ends on custom domains, all powered by the same backbone. One platform, many storefronts. Every new client multiplies the network.

Section 8

Why This Compounds

  • Every event generates data. That data makes SharedReports more valuable. Better reports attract more sponsors. More sponsors fund more events. The flywheel accelerates.
  • Every product feeds another. ourPulse brings organizers to ourSharedEvents. ourSharedEvents powers OurRecNights. OurRecNights creates data for ourEventRings. The ecosystem is the product.
  • Every white-label client multiplies the network. ourSharedMagnets means each new franchise doesn't dilute the platform — it strengthens it. More storefronts, more events, more data, deeper moat.
  • Anonymous data is a feature, not a limitation. Competitors collect PII and inherit liability. We collect patterns and inherit trust. The privacy-first approach becomes harder to replicate over time because it's architectural, not policy.
  • Community is the lock-in. ourSharedTable, OurWKRP, bocce leagues — these aren't features you switch away from. They're relationships. The platform holds because the community holds.
