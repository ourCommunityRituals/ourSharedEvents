This is the organizer sign-in for OurSharedEvents. This page is for event organizers: it opens the tools to create events, print posters and read your reports.

Who this is for

Event organizers. Organizer access is by invitation. A sponsor with a private link from an organizer does not sign in here: open that link instead. Attendees and visitors never sign in, and nothing on the public pages names them.

What happens next

  1. Press Sign in. Cloudflare Access asks for your email address.
  2. It emails you a one-time code. Enter the code.
  3. You land on your events. OurSharedEvents never sees or stores a password; there is none.
Sign in to admin

Locked out, or not an organizer?

Locked out: use the email address you were invited with, and the latest email, because codes expire. If it still fails, ask the person who invited you to check your invitation. There is no password to reset.

Not an organizer: you do not need to sign in to look around. Browse events, or see how OurSharedEvents works.
