# Tracking audit: ourSharedEvents (2026-10-04)

Scope: `workers/oursharedevents/src`, git-tracked files on the local main checkout.

## Summary

- No third-party analytics SDK is present. No Segment, Amplitude, Mixpanel, PostHog, Google Analytics or Workers Analytics Engine calls were found.
- Measurement is first-party: 7 user-triggered measures write to Cloudflare D1, and 4 materializers derive aggregates from them. All 7 are LIVE. 0 are orphaned.
- No identify or group calls. A visitor carries an anonymous cookie id. Tenancy is a `brand_id` with parent/child structure (`node_hierarchy`, `admin_brand_memberships`).
- Aggregates export to per-environment BigQuery datasets (`oursharedevents_backup`, `_stg`, `_dev`).

## Observed patterns

- Each measure has its own table, and there is no single event stream or shared event schema.
- The scan path is the only one buffered through a queue. Beacons and counters write straight to D1.
- Sponsor beacons pass a bot, replay and cadence filter (`classifyBeacon`, lib/invalid-traffic.ts) before insert.
- The scan's placement and anonymous halves go into separate rows that share no durable key (OSE-798).
- Location on sponsor beacons is `h3_res7`. Scans attribute to the poster deployment, not the device.
- Shortlink clicks are a counter plus last-clicked time. There is no per-click row.
- Impressions and clicks are fired from 4 templates: public, display (TV), poster and admin.
- No `event_correlation_id` or `venue_canonical_id` is written by any measure.

## Group hierarchy

The data has a parent/child tenant hierarchy (brand to child brands, event to venue to corridor). Of the third-party tools, Segment, Mixpanel and PostHog model multi-level groups natively, while Amplitude needs workarounds. Here, though, hierarchy lives in OCR's own tables.
